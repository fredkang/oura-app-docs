Privacy Policy

My Claude App — personal Oura Ring integration. Last updated: 2026.

This application ("the App") is a private, single-user integration operated by an individual for their own personal use. This policy explains how it handles data.

What data the App accesses

With the account owner's explicit authorization through Oura's OAuth2 consent screen, the App reads the owner's own Oura Ring data — such as sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, SpO2, stress, workouts, sessions, tags, and related health metrics — via the official Oura API.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to let the account owner review and ask questions about their own health metrics through a personal assistant. It is used only for the owner's benefit.

Storage and sharing

Retrieved data is stored locally on the owner's own devices. The App does not sell, rent, or share the data with any third party, and does not use it for advertising or profiling. No data is made available to anyone other than the account owner.

Access revocation

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time from their Oura account settings, which immediately stops any further data retrieval.
