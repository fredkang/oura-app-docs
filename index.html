My Claude App

A personal Oura Ring integration.

This is a private, single-user application that connects to the Oura API to let its owner query their own Oura Ring health data (sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, and related metrics) through a personal AI assistant. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to other users.

Data is retrieved only for the account owner, stored locally on the owner's own devices, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
